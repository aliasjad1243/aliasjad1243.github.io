Hey, I’m Ali.

I’m a Computer Engineering student at Drexel University. I started out in finance and business analytics before switching to engineering.

These days I split my time between a Formula SAE electric race car, a robotics lab, a brain-computer interface research group, NASA Lunabotics, and building small devices of my own.

I like work where I can hold the result in my hands — and I still bring the analyst habit of checking every result against real numbers.
